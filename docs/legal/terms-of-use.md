## What Sota Is

Sota is a messenger designed for private communication, direct peer-to-peer communication, relay-assisted delivery, and offline nearby mesh communication using technologies such as Bluetooth and Wi-Fi.

Sota is currently in beta. Some features may fail, be delayed, or behave differently depending on device model, Android version, battery restrictions, network conditions, carrier restrictions, and nearby device availability.

## No Emergency Use

Sota is not an emergency service. Do not rely on Sota to contact emergency services, police, ambulance, rescue services, military command, or any other life-critical service.

Offline mesh communication can be useful when internet access is unavailable, but delivery is not guaranteed.
